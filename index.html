How to Play

  1. Tap bars to pick them up. Picked bars lift and glow amber. Tap again to put one back.
  2. Click Strike Out to strike the picked bars. They burn out with a red slash and can't be used again.
  3. Take turns. On your turn you must strike at least one bar. You may strike as many as you like — but they must all be side by side in the same row.
  4. Whoever strikes the very last bar loses. Force your opponent into taking it!

What's allowed — and what isn't

✓ Neighbours in one row. Any run of side-by-side bars — one, two, or the whole row.
✓ Striking in the middle is fine. It splits the row into two separate groups for later turns.
✗ No gaps. 1st and 3rd bar with the 2nd left standing — the bars must touch.
✗ No jumping over struck bars. A burnt bar breaks the row — the bars on either side of it are no longer neighbours.
✗ One row per turn. You can't mix bars from different rows in a single strike.
